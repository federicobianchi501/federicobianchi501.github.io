I am an AI/ML researcher with a Ph.D. in Computer Science, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, from the [Department of Computer Science at the University of Verona](https://www.di.univr.it/?lang=en). My research lies at the intersection of reinforcement learning, planning, learning under uncertainty, and reliable machine learning.

I joined the [ISLa - Intelligent Systems Lab](https://isla-lab.github.io/) in 2018, after completing a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science and a Master's Degree in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Verona. Before and during my Ph.D., I worked on several academic and industrial research projects, including European-funded initiatives and collaborations with industrial partners in areas such as energy and sustainability, defense and security, and chemical-pharmaceutical process modeling.

My Ph.D. research focused on safe policy improvement, model-based reinforcement learning, and Monte Carlo Tree Search, with particular attention to learning and planning under model and data uncertainty. My recent work extends these topics to continuous offline reinforcement learning, uncertainty calibration, and statistically grounded mechanisms for reliable policy improvement. This research resulted in the paper *Calibrated Safe Policy Improvement for Continuous Offline Reinforcement Learning*, accepted at NeurIPS 2026.

Alongside methodological research, I work on applied machine learning problems involving predictive modeling, time-series analysis, process modeling, and data-driven optimization in real-world industrial settings.

**Research Interests**

My current research interests include:

- Reinforcement Learning
- Offline and Model-Based Reinforcement Learning
- Safe Policy Improvement
- Planning and Monte Carlo Tree Search
- Uncertainty Quantification and Calibration
- Reliable and Data-Efficient Machine Learning
- Predictive Modeling and Time-Series Analysis
- Machine Learning for Real-World Industrial Applications

As a member of the ISLa - Intelligent Systems Lab, I contribute to research on both methodological and applied aspects of artificial intelligence, machine learning, planning, and data analysis for intelligent systems.

For further information about my research, publications, and ongoing projects, feel free to contact me.
