I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science, specializing in Artificial Intelligence at the [Department of Computer Science at the University of Verona](https://www.di.univr.it/?lang=en). In 2021, I was awarded a Ph.D. fellowship funded by [Leonardo S.p.A](https://www.leonardo.com/en/home). Previously, in 2018, I joined the [ISLa - Intelligent Systems Lab](https://isla-lab.github.io/) research group, after completing a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science and a Master's Degree in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Verona. Before starting a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence, I was awarded two Research fellowships and I worked as a Temporary Research Fellow at the University of Verona, where I collaborated on projects funded by the [Veneto Region](https://www.regione.veneto.it/). These projects involved numerous Italian universities and companies and focused on [Global House Thermal and Electrical Energy Management](https://www.di.univr.it/?ent=progetto&id=5233&lang=en), as well as [IoT systems for healthy and safe living environments](https://www.ict4ssl.com/en/progetto-safe-place-en/).

**Research Interests**

My research focuses on the development of scalable safe reinforcement learning methods in real-world critical scenarios, which is a research topic concerning problems where a minimum level of performance must be guaranteed while avoiding catastrophic events. In this topic, my interests focus on both single-agent and multi-agent scenarios involved in the environment. Furthermore, I am focusing on strategic planning methods based on Monte Carlo Tree Search in discrete and continuous spaces. As a member of the ISLa - Intelligent Systems Lab, a research group located at the Department of Computer Science at the University of Verona, our research focuses on both methodological and applied aspects of AI, machine learning, and data analysis for intelligent systems. We delve into diverse methodologies ranging from reinforcement learning and uncertainty-aware planning to multi-agent coordination, probabilistic modelling, and statistical data analysis. For more details, feel free to contact me.
